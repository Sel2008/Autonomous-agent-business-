# Autonomous Business Agent

A separate project from Sel2008/Earning-Manager.

## Mission
Turn a business goal into a controlled workflow that can research opportunities, plan work, execute permitted actions, verify results, record actual revenue/costs, and learn from outcomes.

## First business experiments
1. AI-assisted local-business lead research and outreach preparation.
2. Repeatable micro-service delivery.
3. Legitimate online task opportunities.

The service experiments are intentionally zero-capital at the start. This is a testable business model, not a promise of success.

## Controls
- No fabricated earnings.
- No fake identities/accounts/reviews/engagement.
- No CAPTCHA, KYC, access-control or rate-limit bypass.
- No silent financial actions.
- Owner approval for account-level and irreversible actions.
- Evidence and audit trail for material claims.

## Roadmap
MVP dashboard → persistent task/evidence ledger → opportunity verification/scoring → approval center → execution tools → earnings reconciliation → auditor → manager/agent orchestration → optional specialist agents.
